Questions we hear most

What areas do you cover? +

We cover I-10 from Tonopah to Quartzsite, plus Blythe, Wickenburg and the US-60 corridor around Salome.

Do you fix semi truck tires on the road? +

Yes. We repair and replace steer, drive and trailer tires at the roadside or at a truck stop.

How fast can you get to me? +

Typical arrival is 30 to 45 minutes. The exact time depends on where you are on the corridor and road conditions. We confirm it when you call.
