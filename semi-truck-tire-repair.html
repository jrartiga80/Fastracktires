What to do when a tire fails on the highway

  1. Get as far off the road as you safely can, turn on your hazard lights and set out your warning triangles.
  2. Stay with your truck.
  3. Note which tire failed (steer, drive or trailer, and which side) and the tire size printed on the sidewall.
  4. Call (928) 598-1175 and give us your mile marker or exit. We confirm your arrival estimate on the call.
