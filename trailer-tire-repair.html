Trailer tire service

Trailer tire repair for every type of trailer

We repair and replace trailer tires on the shoulder, at a truck stop or in your yard. When a tire can be repaired safely, we plug or patch it. When it cannot, we swap it so the trailer keeps moving.

  • Dry van and reefer trailers. Single and dual tire positions.
  • Flatbed trailers. Including multi-axle setups.
  • Livestock trailers. Quick service so animals are not left waiting on the shoulder.
  • Flats and blowouts. Including damage from road debris.
  • Rim and wheel checks. We change the tire on the rim and tell you honestly if the wheel is safe to keep using.

What to do when a trailer tire fails

  1. Get as far off the road as you safely can, turn on your hazard lights and set out your warning triangles.
  2. Stay with your vehicle.
  3. Note which position failed (axle and side) and the tire size printed on the sidewall.
  4. Call (928) 598-1175 and give us your mile marker or exit.
